    File Types We Repair

    Images

    Format Common Damage What We Recover
    JPEG Header corruption, truncated files Full image (with reference photo) or partial (top portion)
    PNG Header corruption, truncated files Full or partial recovery
    WebP Corruption, won't open Partial recovery
    GIF Header damage, frame errors Partial recovery

    Images have the highest success rate. Even severely damaged photos often recover partially — you may get the top half of the image even if the bottom is lost.

    Documents

    Format Common Damage What We Recover
    PDF Broken headers, damaged structure Full repair (header damage), difficult if severely truncated
    DOCX Won't open, corruption on save Text content recovered — formatting may be lost
    XLSX Won't open, corrupted cells Data content recovered
    ODT Corruption, truncation Text content recovered

    For documents: even when we can't restore perfect formatting, we can almost always recover the text content — which is what matters most.

    Video

    Format Common Damage What We Recover
    MP4 Camera died during recording, incomplete transfer Video recovered up to the break point, audio may be lost
    AVI Header damage, index corruption Partial or full recovery
    MKV Container corruption Partial recovery

    Video repair has a lower success rate but high emotional value — weddings, birthdays, once-in-a-lifetime moments. We will always try.

    Audio

    Format Common Damage What We Recover
    MP3 Truncated, header damage Partial or full recovery (header damage is highly repairable)
    WAV Header corruption, truncated Partial or full recovery
    FLAC Corruption Partial recovery

    Archives

    Format Common Damage What We Recover
    ZIP Broken central directory, truncated Partial extraction — intact files recovered
    R7Z Corruption Partial extraction
    7Z Corruption Partial extraction

    What We Cannot Do (Yet)

    • Physical hard drive recovery — dead drives, clicking drives, RAID recovery
    • Encrypted file recovery — we cannot bypass passwords or encryption
    • Overwritten data — if new data has overwritten the file, recovery is not possible

    !!! info "Planning for the future" Physical drive recovery and forensic services are planned once we acquire the necessary equipment. Watch this space.

    Important: What You Need To Send Us

    For us to repair your file, you must be able to send us the file — even if it's corrupted and won't open on your device.

    If you cannot copy, attach, or upload the file at all (because the device is failing, the file is completely inaccessible, or the drive is making strange noises), that requires physical data recovery — a different service that we do not yet offer.

    !!! info "Physical data recovery coming soon" We are planning to offer physical drive recovery and forensic services in the future once we acquire the necessary equipment. If you need this now, contact us and we can recommend a specialist.
