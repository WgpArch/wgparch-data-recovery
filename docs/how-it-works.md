# How It Works

## Step 1 — Submit Your File

Upload your corrupted file via our [contact page](contact.md).  
Include your name, email, and a brief description of what happened  
(e.g. "file won't open", "download was interrupted", "SD card error").

You will receive a **reference number** by email.

## Step 2 — Pay Analysis Fee

Pay the **R25 analysis fee** using your reference number:

- **Bank:** GoTyme Bank
- **Account:** 51099967550
- **Reference:** Your reference number (e.g. WGP-2610-003)
- **Amount:** R25.00

The R25 fee is **non-refundable**. It covers our time to expertly
examine your file — whether or not it can be repaired.

## Step 3 — We Analyze

Once we receive your R25 payment, we analyze your file and determine:

- What type of damage occurred
- How much content is recoverable
- Whether a full or partial repair is possible

We report back to you by email — usually within **24–48 hours**.

## Step 4 — Repair & Deliver (if repairable)

If your file is repairable, pay the **R100 repair fee** using the
same reference number. Once received, we repair your file and
send it back to you by email or download link.

If your file is **not repairable**, no further payment is required.
You've only spent R25 for an honest expert attempt.

## Important Notes

- **Maximum 5 files per submission**
- **We only accept files from South African customers**
- **Payment by EFT/bank transfer only**
- Your files are deleted from our systems after delivery
